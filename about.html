Our Story

From a frustrated farmer's market visitor to an organic produce movement — this is how FARMFRESH began.

How It Started

Born from Frustration

In 2019, we were a group of friends who loved to cook but hated what the supermarket had become. Produce wrapped in plastic, shipped from thousands of miles away, picked before it was ripe. We knew there had to be a better way.

One Saturday morning at the farmer's market, we met Maria — a third-generation organic farmer who grew the best tomatoes any of us had ever tasted. She told us her biggest challenge wasn't growing the food — it was getting it to people who wanted it.

That conversation sparked an idea: what if we could build a direct bridge between farmers like Maria and families like ours? No warehouses, no middlemen, no food sitting in cold storage for weeks. Just straight from the field to your door.

Our Mission

Honest Food, Honestly Delivered

FARMFRESH exists to prove that fresh, organic produce doesn't have to be a luxury. By cutting out the supply chain middlemen, we pay farmers fairly and charge customers reasonably. Everyone wins — especially the food.

We partner exclusively with certified organic farms within 100 miles of our delivery zones. Every item in our catalog has a story — you can see exactly which farm grew it, when it was harvested, and how it got to you.

Our goal is simple: make it effortless for families to eat food that's genuinely good — for them, for their communities, and for the planet.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every decision we make, from farm selection to last-mile delivery.

🌱

100% Organic

Every product we carry is certified organic. No exceptions, no shortcuts, no "conventional" options hiding in the catalog.

🤝

Fair Partnership

Farmers set their own prices. We take a fair commission and handle logistics. No haggling, no exploitation.

🌍

Local First

All our farms are within 100 miles. Lower carbon footprint, fresher produce, and stronger local economies.

📦

Zero Waste Packaging

All packaging is compostable or reusable. We're building a circular system, not a landfill.

🔍

Full Transparency

Every product page shows the farm, the harvest date, and the journey to your door. No mystery about what you're eating.

❤️

Community Impact

2% of every order supports local food banks and school nutrition programs. Fresh food is a right, not a privilege.
