Frequently Asked

Common Questions

How fast is delivery? +

Most orders are delivered within 24 hours of harvest. We operate delivery routes Monday through Saturday, with next-day delivery available for orders placed before 6 PM.

What if I'm not satisfied? +

We offer a 100% freshness guarantee. If anything doesn't meet your expectations, contact us within 48 hours and we'll either replace it or give you a full credit.

Can I become a FARMFRESH partner farm? +

Absolutely. We're always looking for certified organic farms within our delivery zones. Reach out through our contact form with details about your farm and we'll be in touch to discuss partnership opportunities.

Do you offer wholesale pricing? +

Yes. We offer volume discounts for restaurants, catering companies, and corporate offices. Contact us with your requirements and we'll create a custom pricing plan for you.
